# Themes

**Last updated:** October 14, 2026

Each product has a theme: its colors, type, motion and art. Spacing, sizes and corners come from the shared base, so they're the same in every theme. Each product's own docs only say which theme it uses and link here.

Every theme is a kind of light. To see one, pick it under **Theme** in [Storybook](https://designsystemskeleton.netlify.app/)'s toolbar.

| Theme | Product | Folder | Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Spotlight](#spotlight) | After Graduation | `tokens/themes/after-graduation` | Light and dark |
| [Sunrise](#sunrise) | Life Hub | `tokens/themes/life-hub` | Light and dark |
| [Fireside](#fireside) | Designer toolkit | `tokens/themes/designer-toolkit` | Always dark |

Adding one? See [Add a theme](Add-a-theme).

## Spotlight

After Graduation: a show about getting ready for life after high school, told in seasons and episodes.

- **Color:** quiet by default; color only communicates. One crimson red (#CB1B2C) for the main action, focus and the header's light. Gold is the second color. Dark mode is warm charcoal.
- **Type:** Cinzel for the main title, used once per page like a logo. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything else.
- **Motion:** energetic: things arrive with a little bounce. Everyday pages like Settings can switch to calm.
- **Art:** a red header with a wheel and rays that light the glass tiles over it.
- **Decisions:** crimson (Oct 7), warm charcoal dark mode (Oct 7), type (Oct 4), page shell (Oct 4), quiet by default (Sep 26). All in [Decisions](Decisions).

## Sunrise

Life Hub: one place for every part of Sharon's life, in eight areas.

- **Color:** warm off-white pages. Each area has its own color family and marble background, from the Figma Life Hub Library. Buttons are a warm near-black.
- **Type:** Fraunces for titles (its Soft axis at full), Figtree for everything else.
- **Motion:** calm.
- **Art:** a marble texture per area, and a sky header that follows the time of day ([exploration](https://designsystemskeleton.netlify.app/?path=/story/explorations-life-hub-sky-header--live)). Glass cards sit on the marble, lit from one sun.
- **Decisions:** Life Hub's new look (Oct 7), glass for controls only (Sep 27). All in [Decisions](Decisions).

## Fireside

The designer toolkit: a shell that frames other prototypes, so it stays out of their way.

- **Color:** Retro. **Peach copper (#E1A36F) is the primary color**, used rarely: the main button, focus and the selected item. Behind it: a deep teal-black page (#08161A), teal panels, cream text and soft yellow for supporting text.
- **Always dark,** so the prototype it frames is the brightest thing on screen.
- **Type:** not chosen yet. It borrows Spotlight's fonts and type for now.
- **Motion:** borrows Spotlight's for now.
- **Glass:** not designed yet.
- **Decisions:** Fireside is the third theme, in Retro's colors (Oct 14), in [Decisions](Decisions). The research and references are on the toolkit's [Skin page](https://github.com/sharonlafleur1984/designer-toolkit/wiki/Skin).
